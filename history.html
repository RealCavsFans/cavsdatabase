              Two-way

              3 of 3 spots filled

                Two limits apply, and the tighter one wins: a player needs 3 or fewer years of NBA service to sign a two-way, and he can spend no more than 3 seasons on two-ways with the same team. Tap a player for his details.

                Exhibit 10

                  Exhibit 10 deals are one-year, non-guaranteed minimum contracts for training camp. If the Cavs waive one of these players before the season and he signs with the Charge, Cleveland keeps his G League rights (an affiliate player) and he can earn a bonus of up to $91,000 after 60 days there.

                    Drafted players

                    Unsigned draft rights held Playing Retired

                    Why keep rights to a retired player? Mostly for trades. Every trade has to send something out, and unsigned draft rights count as an asset worth $0 in salary. If the Cavs want a player without giving up anyone under contract, they can send a drafted player’s rights and take the incoming salary into a trade exception, as Cleveland did with Vladimir Veremeenko’s rights in 2018. Holding the rights costs nothing, and only the Cavs can sign the player if he ever comes to the NBA. A team keeps them until it trades or renounces them.
