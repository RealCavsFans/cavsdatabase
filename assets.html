                How it works: Signs free agents for up to four seasons, or takes in players by trade or waiver claim, up to this amount in total. Using it hard caps a team at the first apron. The one exception: if a team only uses it to sign free agents, for no more than the taxpayer mid-level amount and two seasons, it counts as the taxpayer mid-level and hard caps the team at the second apron instead.

                How it works: Created when the Cavs sent out more salary than they took back. It lets them take in a player by trade or waiver claim earning up to this amount plus $250,000 (as long as they stay under the first apron) without sending salary back. It can’t be combined with other salaries and expires one year after the trade. Because it was created in the offseason, using it after the 2026–27 regular season ends would hard cap the Cavs at the first apron for 2027–28 (and for the rest of 2026–27 if used before July 1). They are projected to start 2027–28 over that line.

                Using part of it: It doesn’t have to be used all at once. The Cavs can take in one or several players whose salaries each fit, and whatever is left stays available until it expires. Nothing is lost by using only part of it now. It can’t be combined with other salary or another exception to take in a bigger contract.

              Drafted players

              Unsigned draft rights held Playing Retired

              Why keep rights to a retired player? Mostly for trades. Every trade has to send something out, and unsigned draft rights count as an asset worth $0 in salary. If the Cavs want a player without giving up anyone under contract, they can send a drafted player’s rights and take the incoming salary into a trade exception, as Cleveland did with Vladimir Veremeenko’s rights in 2018. Holding the rights costs nothing, and only the Cavs can sign the player if he ever comes to the NBA. A team keeps them while the player plays elsewhere. If he tells the team he is ready to sign, it keeps them for one more year. It can also trade or renounce them at any time.
