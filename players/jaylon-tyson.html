                Drafted players

                Unsigned draft rights held Playing Retired

                Why keep rights to a retired player? Mostly for trades. Every trade has to send something out, and unsigned draft rights count as an asset worth $0 in salary. If the Cavs want a player without giving up anyone under contract, they can send a drafted player’s rights and take the incoming salary into a trade exception, as Cleveland did with Vladimir Veremeenko’s rights in 2018. Holding the rights costs nothing, and only the Cavs can sign the player if he ever comes to the NBA. A team keeps them while the player plays elsewhere. If he tells the team he is ready to sign, it keeps them for one more year. It can also trade or renounce them at any time.

                  How the numbers work

                  From the final buzzer to this site, in five steps.

                  1. 1CollectOfficial NBA game filesAfter every game, the NBA's official box score and every play are pulled straight from the league's own files. ESPN fills in if those are late, and the official injury report is read every 30 minutes on game days.
                  2. 2StoreOne copy of every gameOne copy of every game, box score and play is saved, so every page (live, season, players, scouting, ranks) reads from the same games and the same player ids.
                  3. 3CalculateSame formulas for all 30 teamsThe same formulas run for the Cavs and all 30 teams, this season and last, so a Cavs number and its league rank always come from the same place. Ratings and pace use possessions counted from the play-by-play, the way NBA.com counts them. Each stat's definition sits right next to it on the site.
                  4. 4CheckMatched to the box scoreEach night every game is rebuilt from the play-by-play and matched to the official box score, player by player. Possessions are counted from the play-by-play and checked each morning against NBA.com’s official counts. Every box score is also checked against ESPN’s copy, every +/- against the NBA’s, and a daily health check makes sure every feed is still updating.
                  5. 5PublishUpdated after every gamePages update automatically after each game; the live page refreshes every 15 seconds. Spot something off? Let us know below.
