                Drafted players

                Unsigned draft rights held Playing Retired

                Why keep rights to a retired player? Mostly for trades. Every trade has to send something out, and unsigned draft rights count as an asset worth $0 in salary. If the Cavs want a player without giving up anyone under contract, they can send a drafted player’s rights and take the incoming salary into a trade exception, as Cleveland did with Vladimir Veremeenko’s rights in 2018. Holding the rights costs nothing, and only the Cavs can sign the player if he ever comes to the NBA. A team keeps them while the player plays elsewhere. If he tells the team he is ready to sign, it keeps them for one more year. It can also trade or renounce them at any time.
