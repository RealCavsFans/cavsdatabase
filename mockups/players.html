Player pages: how someone gets to one

Nothing about today’s pages goes away. Each player just gets one more place to land, with its own link.

1From Google or the site’s search

Someone searches “Evan Mobley contract” and the player page is the result. On the site, typing his name in search now offers his page first.

2From any name on the site

Roster, cap sheet, depth chart and Transactions: his name gets a gold underline and links to his page, and the card gets a “Full player page →” link.

3The page itself

Everything on one screen: photo, bio, contract by season, stats, depth chart spot, combine numbers, trade-eligible date, and links back to each section. Share and save-as-image work like everywhere else.

  • No new tab in the menu. Player pages are reached from names and search, so the top nav stays the same.
  • About 20 pages (current roster, two-ways and camp players), built automatically from the data the site already has. When a player leaves, his page stays up, marked as a former Cavalier.
  • The photo is blank in this mockup only because the sample page couldn’t load NBA.com images here; the real page uses his headshot.
