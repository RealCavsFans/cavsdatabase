Schedule & rest analysis

Ideas for a "Schedule analysis" section on the Schedule page, built from the NBA's 2026–27 schedule (80 games; the 2 Cup games in December aren't set yet). Every number below is real. Rest = days off before a game. "Rested vs tired" = the Cavs had at least a day off and the opponent is on the second night of a back-to-back.

Idea 1 · schedule report card

Six numbers at the top of the section, each with where the Cavs rank among 30 teams (1st = toughest).

Idea 2 · rest calendar

The whole season at a glance. Each game day is colored by how much rest the Cavs had: red = second night of a back-to-back, pink = one day off, green = two or more. The white dot = home. A gold outline marks games where the Cavs are rested and the opponent is tired; a black outline is the reverse.
